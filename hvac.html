Your busiest hour is also your worst one

The calls that matter arrive when you are least able to take them. First cold snap, first heat wave, a Saturday. You are inside a unit with your hands full and the phone is in the van.

A booked appointment that no-shows costs you a truck roll, a technician's time, and a hole in the day you cannot refill, so the reminder before the slot matters as much as the reply that booked it.

Harvard Business Review audited 2,241 US companies and found 23 percent never answered a web enquiry at all, with an average reply of 42 hours. A homeowner without heat does not wait 42 hours.

Oldroyd, McElheran and Elkington, The Short Life of Online Sales Leads, Harvard Business Review 89(3), March 2011.

What gets handled

Four kinds of enquiry, one sequence

  • 01 Emergency calls. No heat, no cool air, water where it should not be. These get answered first and routed by how far the job is from whoever is already out.
  • 02 Quotes and replacements. A fifteen year old system that finally quit is a different conversation from a repair, and it gets a different set of qualifying questions.
  • 03 Maintenance and seasonal work. The enquiries that arrive in the quiet months and get forgotten, chased on the same sequence as everything else.
  • 04 Last season's dead leads. The estimates nobody followed up on in February. They get worked when the first hot week arrives and the intent comes back.

The websites, local search and AI visibility that produce these enquiries are the other half of what we do.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it know the difference between a no-heat call and a quote request?

Yes. The first message asks what the enquiry is about before anything else, and an emergency is routed differently from a replacement quote. The qualifying questions change with the answer.

What happens to leads outside my service radius?

They never reach your calendar. You give us the radius and anything outside it is answered honestly and closed, rather than booked and then cancelled the day before.

Do I need to change how my technicians work?

No. Bookings land in the calendar you already use and the messages go out under your own number. What changes is that somebody already qualified the job before the van is assigned.
