Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ProAffy ("ProAffy", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website or contact us. ProAffy is a marketing and growth partner for HVAC companies in the United States. By using this website, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to give us. When you fill out a form on this site or email us, we may collect your name, email address, company name, and any details you include in your message. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not sell products directly through this website.

Like most websites, we may also automatically receive basic technical information such as your browser type, device, and general location, along with anonymous analytics about how visitors use the site. This helps us understand what is useful and improve the experience.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry, schedule and deliver your free pilot, communicate with you about our services, and improve our website and offerings. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

How We Share Information

We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate our business, such as email, hosting, scheduling, and analytics tools. These providers only receive the information needed to perform their work and are expected to protect it. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Cookies and Analytics

This site may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences and measure traffic. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect how some parts of the site function.

Data Retention

We keep the information you provide for as long as needed to respond to you, deliver our services, and meet legal or business requirements. You may ask us to delete your information at any time using the contact details below.

Your Choices and Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you, and you may opt out of marketing emails at any time. To make a request, email us at the address below and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for business owners and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be reflected here.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, email us at info@proaffy.com.
