About ProAffy

We're Not a Chatbot Company.
We're an HVAC Growth Partner.

At ProAffy, we focus on one thing: helping HVAC companies book more jobs and stop losing money on leads that slip through the cracks.

The Problem We Kept Seeing

We saw the same problem over and over again. HVAC owners spending real money on ads and getting real leads, but losing a huge chunk of them simply because nobody followed up fast enough, or at all.

Calls go to voicemail. Texts go unanswered for hours. Old leads pile up in a CRM and never get touched again. That's not a marketing problem. That's a follow-up problem. And it's costing HVAC companies thousands every single month.

So We Built the ThermoGrowth Engine

A complete system that fills your pipeline with leads, responds to every single one within 60 seconds, books qualified jobs straight into your calendar, and follows up automatically until a lead either books or says no.
