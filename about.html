About ProAffy

The gap between a lead arriving and somebody answering it

That gap is where the money goes

An HVAC owner spends real money on ads and gets real enquiries. Then a furnace call runs long, the phone stays in the van, and by evening four of those enquiries have rung somebody else. Nothing was neglected. The work simply happened at the same time as the enquiries did.

It is measurable, and other people have measured it. Harvard Business Review audited 2,241 US companies and found that 23 percent never answered a web enquiry at all, and that the average reply took 42 hours.

So that is the only thing we work on: what happens in the minutes after an enquiry arrives, and whether anybody is there to handle it.

Oldroyd, McElheran and Elkington, The Short Life of Online Sales Leads, Harvard Business Review 89(3), March 2011.

How we work

Three things we hold ourselves to

  • 01 We run it, you do not. There is no new software for your office to learn and no extra tool to watch. Messages go out under your own number, and bookings land wherever you already keep them.
  • 02 You see the actual replies. Not a dashboard summarising them. The messages that went out, when they went out, and what came back.
  • 03 The pilot comes first. A month on your live enquiries before anything is agreed, because a month of real replies settles the question and a sales page does not.

What we are not

We do not sell software, and we are not a chatbot. A chatbot answers on your website, where the person already is. The work is reaching the ones who left, on the channel they actually read, before the next company gets to them.

We publish no client results either, because a month of your own enquiries tells you more than an average taken from somebody else's.
