You are quoted against three other companies

A homeowner requesting a quote has almost always requested three more. The one who answers first gets to frame what matters: roof orientation, shading, what the utility will actually pay for an export.

Then the cycle is long. A site survey, a system design, an interconnection application, a payback figure they want to check with somebody. Most of that happens after the enthusiasm has worn off, which is exactly when follow-up stops in most companies.

Of 2,241 US companies audited by Harvard Business Review, 23 percent never replied to a web enquiry at all. Against three other installers that is not a slow start, it is an absence.

Oldroyd, McElheran and Elkington, The Short Life of Online Sales Leads, Harvard Business Review 89(3), March 2011.

What gets handled

Four kinds of enquiry, one sequence

  • 01 First contact, before the other three. A reply in under a minute, asking the two things that decide whether a site survey is worth anybody's afternoon: the roof and the bill.
  • 02 The long middle. Follow-up that keeps going across weeks, not hours, because that is how long the decision takes. It stops when they book or tell you no.
  • 03 Survey and design appointments. Booked into your crew's calendar with the address and the roof details already attached, and reminded before the slot.
  • 04 Quotes that went quiet. The proposals sent three months ago that never got a yes or a no. Rates change, incentives change, and the conversation is worth reopening.

Getting found by homeowners who are comparing installers is the other half of what we do.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Our sales cycle is weeks, not minutes. Does a 60 second reply matter?

It decides whether there is a cycle at all. The homeowner is contacting several installers in one sitting, and the first useful answer sets the terms of every quote that follows. The long follow-up is the other half of what we run.

Can it qualify a roof before booking a survey?

It asks the questions that rule a site out cheaply: roof age and orientation, obvious shading, whether they own the property, and roughly what they pay now. A survey nobody should have driven to is the expensive kind of appointment.

Do you understand interconnection and incentives?

We do not design systems or file applications, and we will not pretend to. We handle the part between an enquiry arriving and a qualified appointment reaching your crew. Everything after that is yours.
